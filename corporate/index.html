ABOUT AVTR

Built for Amman.
Designed for the future.

AVTR was founded to help transform waste-management services and expand recycling solutions in Amman. Its role connects day-to-day city operations with a longer-term transition toward more sustainable, efficient and circular systems.

The company’s direction brings together innovation, strategic partnerships, environmental responsibility, international best practices and community engagement—turning waste from a challenge into an opportunity for better services and greater resource value.

FEATURED PROJECT

Al Ghabawi.
Gas to energy.

AVTR owns and operates the Amman Ghabawi Landfill Gas to Energy Project. Landfill gas is captured and recovered methane is used to generate electricity for the national grid.

This project connects responsible landfill management with climate action, renewable energy, air-quality improvement and more efficient use of resources.
