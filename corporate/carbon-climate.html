LANDFILL GAS METHANE CAPTURE ELECTRICITY
AL GHABAWI • CASE STUDY

Project 3370.
Methane into power.

AVTR owns and operates the Amman Ghabawi Landfill Gas to Energy Project at Al Ghabawi sanitary landfill, approximately 40 km east of Amman. The facility captures landfill gas and uses recovered methane to generate electricity supplied to Jordan’s national grid.
