HOW DELIVERY WORKS
------------------
1) EMAIL + PHOTO
   The form uses FormSubmit to forward the complaint and uploaded image to:
   MHMDFIRAS01@GMAIL.COM

   IMPORTANT: FormSubmit requires ONE-TIME activation for a new receiving email.
   Before publishing the site, submit one TEST complaint from complaints.html.
   Then open MHMDFIRAS01@GMAIL.COM and click the activation/confirmation email
   sent by FormSubmit. After activation, future form submissions are forwarded.

   IMPORTANT: Activate before accepting real complaints with photos. FormSubmit
   states that submissions can be retained before activation, but uploaded files
   are not retained while waiting for activation.

2) PHOTO
   The photo is attached to the email submission (JPG / PNG / WEBP, max 10 MB).

3) LOCATION
   "Use my current location" uses browser geolocation. The user must approve the
   browser location permission. The coordinates and a Google Maps link are then
   included in the email form data.

4) FOLLOW-UP
   Email is the only configured communication and follow-up channel.
   The complaint form requires the reporter's email address.
