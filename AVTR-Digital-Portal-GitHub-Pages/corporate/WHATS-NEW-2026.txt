AVTR WEBSITE — MODERN INTERACTION & MOBILE UPGRADE

This build keeps the light AVTR visual identity and upgrades the complete website with:

• Full responsive behavior for desktop, tablet and mobile.
• Improved mobile navigation with open/close animation and ESC/outside-click handling.
• Scroll progress indicator at the top of every page.
• Floating Back to Top button.
• Section-aware navigation on the Home page.
• Staggered reveal animations as content enters the viewport.
• Subtle interactive spotlight effect on cards (desktop only).
• Very light 3D card movement (desktop only).
• Magnetic button micro-interactions (desktop only).
• Subtle parallax on major image panels (desktop only).
• Animated numeric statistics on the Home page.
• Better touch targets, font sizes and form controls on phones.
• Responsive layouts for departments, service areas, complaints and content pages.
• Reduced-motion accessibility support.
• Focus-visible keyboard accessibility.
• Lazy loading and async image decoding where appropriate.
• START-AVTR.bat now serves the correct project folder on 0.0.0.0:8000.

PHONE TESTING
1. Double-click START-AVTR.bat.
2. For a public HTTPS phone link, keep the server open and run:
   cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8000
3. Open the generated trycloudflare.com link on the phone.
